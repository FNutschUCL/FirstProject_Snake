# Your first project 🐍

Welcome. In the next few minutes you are going to change a real piece of
software — without writing any code.

You do not need to install anything. You do not need to know what any of
these files are. Just follow the three steps below.

---

## Step 1 — Look at your game

The game should already be running in a preview tab next to this one.

If you can't see it, look for a tab called **"Your Snake game"**, or open
the **Ports** tab at the bottom of the screen and click the little globe
icon next to port 8080.

Click on the game and press an arrow key. That's your snake.

---

## Step 2 — Meet your assistant

On the left edge of the screen there's a row of icons. Click the **Cline**
icon to open your AI assistant.

This is who you'll be talking to. You describe what you want in ordinary
English, and it makes the change for you.

---

## Step 3 — Make it yours

Type this into Cline, exactly as it is:

> make the snake blue

Then press enter, and wait a moment. Cline will tell you what it's doing
and change the file for you.

When it's finished, go back to your game and **refresh the preview**.

Your snake is blue.

---

## That's the hard part done

You just changed software by asking for it. Everything else in this course
builds on the thing you did in those three steps.

When you're ready, try a few more:

- `make the food yellow`
- `make the snake move faster`
- `make the background dark purple`
- `make the snake start out longer`

Nothing you can type will break anything permanently. If something goes
wrong, just ask Cline to undo it.

---

### If you're curious

Everything you're changing lives in a file called `game.js`, in a section
at the top marked `SETTINGS`. You're welcome to open it and look — but you
don't have to. Ask Cline to explain any part of it you're curious about.
