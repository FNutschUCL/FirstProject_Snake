2
Change a colour by hand

Open game.js from the file list. Look at the top, where it says SETTINGS. Find the line starting snakeColour:.

Replace what's inside the quotes with another colour — blue, hotpink, gold. Keep the quotes.

Save with Ctrl+S (Mac: Cmd+S), then hit Reload game.
