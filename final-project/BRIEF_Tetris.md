# Build Tetris

Blocks fall from the top. You move and rotate them. Complete a full row
and it disappears. Miss too often and the stack reaches the top — game
over.

That's the whole description you get. How you get there is yours.

---

## The three requirements

Every finished project must have all three. They're the same for both
routes.

### 1. It remembers something

Close the page, open it again, and something you did is still there —
your high score, your name, your settings. Anything, as long as it
genuinely survives a reload.

### 2. Three parts that do different things

Not three variations of one thing. For example: the falling blocks, a
score that counts, and a preview of the next piece. Or a pause screen, a
level that speeds up, a keyboard control scheme.

### 3. It looks how *you* want it to look

Not how the assistant first decided. At some point you must push back on
the design and ask for something different — bolder, calmer, different
colours, whatever you actually want.

---

## Before you call it finished

Test it properly. Play until you clear a row. Play until you lose.
Reload the page and check requirement 1 really holds.

"It appeared to work once" is not the same as "it works".
